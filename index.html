UIM Progression Guide

Written for Ultimate Ironmen from the first tick, at a comfortable pace, and it never assumes a bank. What it optimises is comfort and safety: no tick manipulation outside Chapter 4's fast track, the fewest trips, slots and risky deaths, and training that quest XP would give you later left to the quests.

Tick each block or step as you finish it. Your progress is saved in this browser, and the button below takes you to the next unticked step.

Chapter 2

The long quest blocks to Song of the Elves

Long thematic blocks, each saying what you carry, what you drop, where you die on purpose and why. It ends with Song of the Elves done and about 34.8 million gp.

Chapter 3

Song of the Elves to the quest cape

From Song of the Elves to a quest cape, 100 combat, the Bow of Faerdhinen and crystal armour, with Construction at 86 and Sailing at 78.

Chapter 4

The quest cape to the max cape

Eight skill blocks from the end of Chapter 3 to the max cape, each with a fast track and an easier option, in an order set by what each block supplies to the next.
